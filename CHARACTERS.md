## Voice Categories Per Character

Each approved character has 6 taunt categories:

| Category | Trigger | Example (Elon Musk) |
|---|---|---|
| **taunt** | Firing a shot | "My rocket lands itself. Yours is about to become a crater." |
| **kill** | Destroying an enemy | "Another one bites the lunar dust! Going straight to X!" |
| **death** | Being destroyed | "The FAA... they finally got me... tell Grimes..." |
| **shop** | Buying a weapon | "Buying more weapons. Funding round secured." |
| **revenge** | Killing the player who previously killed you | "Nobody shoots Elon Musk and gets away with it!" |
| **nearmiss** | Enemy shot lands close but misses | "Your trajectory is as bad as Tesla's Q3 deliveries!" |
